                      THE HALLS OF ZK BY TELEPHONE
                      ============================

Call +61 8 6186 3484.  The game is read aloud by DECtalk and you play
it with your phone's keypad; there is nothing to type.


STARTING
--------

When asked for your passcode, a new player presses # and then:

    1   to start a new game
    2   to hear about the game first

You will be given a six digit passcode, read out twice.  Keep it safe:
it is how you get back to your saved games the next time you call.
Returning players just key in their passcode.

If you also play at a terminal, sign in there and link your phone
account from account maintenance, using your passcode.  Your saved
games are then shared: save at the terminal and restore on the phone,
or the other way about.  A linked account can be played in one place
at a time, so sign out at the terminal before you call.


IN A ROOM
---------

The keypad is a compass.  5 describes the room again and # lists the
ways out.

        +-------+-------+-------+
        |   1   |   2   |   3   |
        |  NW   |   N   |  NE   |
        +-------+-------+-------+
        |   4   |   5   |   6   |
        |   W   | LOOK  |   E   |
        +-------+-------+-------+
        |   7   |   8   |   9   |
        |  SW   |   S   |  SE   |
        +-------+-------+-------+
        |   *   |   0   |   #   |
        |       | MENU  | EXITS |
        +-------+-------+-------+


THE MENU
--------

Press 0 for the menu:

    1   Objects here: pick one, then take, examine, open and so on
    2   Go up, where you can
    3   Inventory: pick an object you carry to use it
    4   Score
    5   Save your game: # keeps its name, 1 gives it a new one
    6   Restore a game: the names are read out, each with a key
    7   Quit
    8   Go down, where you can
    9   Help
    *   Back to the game

A saved game is called PHONE until you name it.  Say the name after
the tone, or spell it on the keypad; what was heard is read back
before it is used.  A name is letters and digits, and cannot be a word
the game itself knows, such as NORTH.


HOW IT WORKS
------------

The game runs on OpenVMS VAX as a FastAGI server.  Calls are answered
by Asterisk on a small Linux box, which connects each caller to the
VAX.  The VAX sends the game's text; the Linux box speaks it with
DECtalk, plays it down the line and passes the caller's keys back.
Only text and key presses cross the network.

ASTERISK.md describes how to set up the Linux side.
